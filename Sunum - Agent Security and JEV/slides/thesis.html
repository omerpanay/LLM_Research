What this means for my thesis

I could not find a published test that checks a car assistant against many-step attacks. So I decided to make a proposal. The two closest tests each cover only one half:

AgentLAB (ICML 2026)CAR-bench (ACL 2026, BMW Group Research)
What it has5 attack types, 28 test environments, 644 test cases58 car tools, 19 rules, 240 tasks, 31 car settings it can check
What it is missingNo carNo attacks
Who decides successAn LLM judge (GPT-5.1)Code for 12 rules, an LLM judge for 7
Tezin boşluğu burada: AgentLAB'de saldırı var ama araç yok, CAR-bench'te araç var ama saldırı yok. İkisini birleştiren yayımlanmış bir çalışma bulamadım. CAR-bench BMW Group Research ve Augsburg Üniversitesi'nden; otomotiv tarafı için ikna edici.
