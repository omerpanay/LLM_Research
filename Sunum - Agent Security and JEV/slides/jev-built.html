4 · JEV

How it is built

TypeSafe hasn’t published how JEV works inside, so I put the picture together from three sources.

SourceWhat it tells us
The company’s own websiteJEV doesn’t write an answer word by word. It reads the information once, then answers all the questions together, with a probability for each option.
An outside test with about 10,000 requestsEach question sees the shared information, but not the other questions. 200 options take as long as 2, and even 1,500 questions are answered in under a second.
Open copies of the idea (Kev, Perplexity Decider)They take a normal open model and add one small layer that scores each option. They get almost the same accuracy as JEV.
Önemli: şirket mimariyi, ağırlıkları ve bir makaleyi yayımlamadı. Bunu sen söyle, güven kazanırsın. Ama üç ayrı kaynak aynı yönü gösteriyor: şirketin kendi sitesi, yaklaşık 10.000 istekle yapılmış bağımsız bir test ve aynı fikri açık kodla yeniden yapan modeller.
